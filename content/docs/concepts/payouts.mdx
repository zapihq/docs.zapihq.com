---
title: Payouts
description: How a Zapi payout moves money from your balance to an onboarded recipient.
---

A payout is money a merchant sends out of its Zapi balance to a recipient,
tracked through its own lifecycle. It is the only money-out object in the API.
There is no `transfers` resource, path, event, or alias.

A recipient is a party you pay out to — a seller, creator, vendor, or the
merchant itself. Onboard each recipient once in the dashboard. The public API
references recipients by their `rcp_...` identifier and does not create or
modify them.

## The lifecycle

```text
pending ──▶ processing ──▶ succeeded
   │            │
   │            ├──▶ failed
   └──cancel──▶ cancelled
```

`pending` means the payout is queued and can still be cancelled. `processing`
means the provider is moving the money; the cancellation window has closed.
`succeeded`, `failed`, and `cancelled` are final.

A failed payout carries a `failure_code`: `insufficient_balance`,
`invalid_destination`, `provider_declined`, or `provider_unavailable`.

## Balance and recipients

A payout debits your Zapi balance. If the balance does not cover the amount, the
payout fails with `insufficient_balance`. Before you send, confirm the recipient
is `active`; an `inactive` recipient cannot receive money.

The API exposes no balance endpoint and no recipient endpoints. Use the
dashboard for both, and the API for moving money.

## Destination

`destination` is always a saved `rcp_...` recipient, never a raw bank account or
wallet number. That keeps account data in the dashboard, where it was onboarded
and verified, instead of in every payout request.

## Failure is not cancellation

A failed payout reached the provider and was refused. A cancelled payout stopped
before the provider picked it up. Both end the payout, but they mean different
things for reconciliation: do not mark a recipient as paid in either case, and
keep the failure code.
