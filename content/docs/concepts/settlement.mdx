---
title: Settlement
description: How captured payments become a merchant balance, and how payouts draw on it.
---

Settlement is the process that makes captured funds available as a merchant
balance. A successful payment is captured from the payer; settlement is what
turns that capture into balance you can pay out.

## Capture and balance

When a payment reaches `succeeded`, the amount is captured. The payment object
records the amount in santim and stays retrievable. A refund against that
payment reduces what the merchant keeps, in whole or in part.

A payout debits the merchant balance. The balance must cover the payout before
the provider is asked to move money; otherwise the payout fails with
`insufficient_balance`.

## What is not published

Settlement timing, fees, and reserve rules are not published in these docs until
a verified source backs them. Do not infer a schedule from a test-mode result, a
marketing figure, or a provider's own timeline. If your reconciliation depends on
a specific availability time, confirm it with Zapi before you build around it.

## Reconciliation

The API gives you the objects, not a settlement ledger:

- List payments and filter by `created_after` and `created_before`.
- List refunds and filter by `payment`.
- List payouts and filter by `destination`.

The dashboard holds the balance view. Reconcile from the objects and the
dashboard rather than from event arrival order; events are delivered at least
once and may arrive late.

## Multi-party settlement

Payouts let one merchant pay recipients from its balance. The merchant remains
the account holder; recipients are destinations, not separate merchant accounts.
There is no split-payment object in the current contract. If you need to divide a
single payment across parties, model each share as a payout after the payment
succeeds.
