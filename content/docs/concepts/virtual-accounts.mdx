---
title: Virtual accounts
description: Account identifiers that route inbound funds to a single merchant.
---

A virtual account is an account identifier that routes inbound funds to a single
merchant. Use one when you need to attribute incoming bank transfers to a
specific merchant, branch, or purpose rather than a shared collection account.

## What a virtual account is

A virtual account looks like an account number to the sender, but it is not a
separate bank account. It is an identifier that tells Zapi which merchant the
incoming transfer belongs to. The funds still settle to the merchant's balance.

Virtual accounts are for collecting money. They are not wallets, sub-accounts, or
payout destinations; use a recipient (`rcp_...`) for payouts.

## In the current contract

The public API does not expose a virtual-account endpoint. The OpenAPI contract
in this repository defines `acct_...` identifiers, but no route creates,
retrieves, or lists them.

Do not build against an endpoint that is not in the contract. If you need a
virtual account today, contact Zapi for the current onboarding process and the
events your integration should expect. When the endpoint ships, it lands in the
contract first and on this page second.

## Reconciliation

Treat an inbound transfer to a virtual account as a payment to the merchant it
belongs to. Reconcile from the identifier, not from the sender's name or
reference, because those are outside your control.
