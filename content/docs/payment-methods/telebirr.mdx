title: telebirr
description: Accept telebirr wallet payments, send payouts to telebirr wallets, and handle the payer flow.

telebirr is a mobile wallet and one lowercase word in prose and payloads. Use
`"payment_method": "telebirr"` to collect a payment from a telebirr wallet, and a
`telebirr` recipient to pay money out to one.

## Collect a payment

Create the payment with `payment_method` set to `telebirr`:

```bash
curl https://api.zapihq.com/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: req_9x8h2m" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 100000,
    "currency": "ETB",
    "payment_method": "telebirr",
    "reference": "order_1042"
  }'
```

The request carries no phone number. The payer approves the payment on the hosted
checkout at `checkout_url`, where Zapi collects the wallet details. When they
finish, Zapi sends `payment.success` or `payment.failed`.

## Payouts

A telebirr recipient has `type: "wallet"` and `provider: "telebirr"`. Reference
its `rcp_...` identifier in `POST /v1/payouts`. Recipients are onboarded in the
dashboard; the public API does not create them.

## Refunds

A succeeded telebirr payment can be refunded in full or in part. The refund
starts `pending` and ends `succeeded` or `failed`. A failed refund leaves the
original payment intact.

## Failure codes

Payment failures use `insufficient_funds`, `payer_cancelled`, `timed_out`,
`provider_declined`, `invalid_account`, and `provider_unavailable`. Read the
`failure_code`; `failure_message` is for humans and may change.

## Fees and timing

Fees and settlement times are not published in these docs until a verified source
backs them. Do not infer them from test-mode behavior.
