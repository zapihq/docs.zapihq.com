---
title: Bank of Abyssinia
description: Accept payments from Bank of Abyssinia accounts and pay BOA recipients.
---

Bank of Abyssinia (BOA) is a bank account method. Use
`"payment_method": "boa"` to collect a payment, and a BOA recipient to pay money
out to an account at the bank.

## Collect a payment

Create the payment with `payment_method` set to `boa`:

```bash
curl https://api.zapihq.com/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: req_9x8h2m" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 100000,
    "currency": "ETB",
    "payment_method": "boa",
    "reference": "order_1042"
  }'
```

The request carries no account number. The payer completes the payment on the
hosted checkout at `checkout_url`, where Zapi collects the bank details. When
they finish, Zapi sends `payment.success` or `payment.failed`.

## Payouts

A BOA recipient has `type: "bank_account"` and `provider: "boa"`. Reference its
`rcp_...` identifier in `POST /v1/payouts`. Recipients are onboarded in the
dashboard; the public API does not create them.

## Refunds

A succeeded BOA payment can be refunded in full or in part. The refund starts
`pending` and ends `succeeded` or `failed`. A failed refund leaves the original
payment intact.

## Failure codes

Payment failures use `insufficient_funds`, `payer_cancelled`, `timed_out`,
`provider_declined`, `invalid_account`, and `provider_unavailable`. Payout
failures use `insufficient_balance`, `invalid_destination`, `provider_declined`,
and `provider_unavailable`.

## Fees and timing

Fees and settlement times are not published in these docs until a verified source
backs them. Do not infer them from test-mode behavior.
