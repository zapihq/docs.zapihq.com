---
title: Payment methods
description: The providers a payer can pay through, and what each one needs.
---

A payment method is the instrument a payer uses; a provider is the institution
that operates it. Zapi routes telebirr wallets, bank accounts, and cards through
one create-payment request, named by `payment_method`.

EthSwitch is an interbank switch that connects the banks. It is not a payment
method and never appears as a `payment_method` value.

## Compare methods

| Method                            | `payment_method` | Type         | Payments | Refunds | Payouts |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------ | -------- | ------- | ------- |
| telebirr                          | `telebirr`       | Wallet       | Yes      | Yes     | Yes     |
| Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) | `cbe`            | Bank account | Yes      | Yes     | Yes     |
| Awash Bank                        | `awash`          | Bank account | Yes      | Yes     | Yes     |
| Bank of Abyssinia (BOA)           | `boa`            | Bank account | Yes      | Yes     | Yes     |
| Dashen Bank                       | `dashen`         | Bank account | Yes      | Yes     | Yes     |
| Card                              | `card`           | Card         | Yes      | Yes     | No      |

"Payouts" means the method can be a recipient's destination. Card recipients are
not supported: a payout goes to a bank account or a telebirr wallet.

Fees and settlement times are not published in these docs until a verified
source backs them.

## The request is the same

Choose the method with `payment_method`; the rest of the create request does not
change. The payer supplies the phone number, bank account, or card on the hosted
checkout, never in your request.

```bash
curl https://api.zapihq.com/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: req_9x8h2m" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 100000,
    "currency": "ETB",
    "payment_method": "cbe",
    "reference": "order_1042"
  }'
```

The response includes `checkout_url` whenever the payer must approve the payment.
Send them there, then wait for `payment.success`.

## Choose a method

- **telebirr** for wallet payments where the payer approves on their phone.
- **Bank accounts** for payers who pay from CBE, Awash, BOA, or Dashen.
- **Card** for card payments. Cards cannot receive payouts.
