---
title: Cards
description: Accept card payments on Zapi's hosted checkout without handling card data.
---

Card is a payment method for collecting money. Use `"payment_method": "card"`.
Card details are collected on Zapi's hosted checkout, not in your request.

Cards cannot receive payouts. A payout destination is a bank account or a
telebirr wallet.

## Collect a payment

```bash
curl https://api.zapihq.com/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: req_9x8h2m" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 100000,
    "currency": "ETB",
    "payment_method": "card",
    "reference": "order_1042"
  }'
```

The request carries no card number, expiry, or security code. The payer enters
them on the hosted checkout at `checkout_url`. That boundary keeps your servers
out of card-data scope.

There is no publishable key and no client-side card tokenisation step in the
current contract. Create the payment on your server and redirect the payer.

## Refunds

A succeeded card payment can be refunded in full or in part. The refund starts
`pending` and ends `succeeded` or `failed`. A failed refund leaves the original
payment intact.

## Failure codes

Payment failures use `insufficient_funds`, `payer_cancelled`, `timed_out`,
`provider_declined`, `invalid_account`, and `provider_unavailable`. For a card,
`provider_declined` usually means the issuer refused the payment, and
`invalid_account` means the card details could not be used.

## Fees and timing

Fees and settlement times are not published in these docs until a verified source
backs them. Do not infer them from test-mode behavior.
