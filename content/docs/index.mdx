Zapi is a payments API for Ethiopian merchants. You send one request, the payer
confirms, and the payment settles to your balance.

The OpenAPI contract in this repository is the source of truth. Samples in prose
are illustrative unless a page says otherwise.

## Start here

- [Guides](/docs/guides) — one job at a time, starting with accepting a payment.
- [Concepts](/docs/concepts) — payments, payouts, settlement, and events.
- [Payment methods](/docs/payment-methods) — telebirr, bank accounts, and cards.
- [API reference](/docs/api-reference) — requests, fields, and responses.
