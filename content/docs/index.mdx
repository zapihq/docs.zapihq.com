Zapi is a payments API for Ethiopian merchants. You send one request, the payer
confirms, and the payment settles to your balance.

The OpenAPI contract in this repository is the source of truth. Samples in prose
are illustrative unless a page says otherwise.

## Start here

- [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart) — create your first test payment.
- [Accept a payment](/docs/guides/accept-a-payment) — take a payment end to end.
- [Send a payout](/docs/guides/send-a-payout) — move money to a bank account.
- [Receive webhooks](/docs/guides/receive-webhooks) — handle payment events.
- [API reference](/docs/api-reference/payments) — requests, fields, and responses.
