A payment method is the instrument a payer uses. A provider is the channel that
executes it. You name both in the create-payment request.

| Payer uses                          | `provider` value |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| telebirr wallet                     | `telebirr`       |
| Commercial Bank of Ethiopia account | `cbe`            |
| Awash Bank account                  | `awash`          |
| Card                                | `card`           |

The provider values above are illustrative until the API spec is published.
`telebirr` is always one lowercase word, in prose and in payloads.

## Choosing a provider

Show the providers your payers actually use. A checkout with four options asks
the payer to choose; a checkout with one option asks them to confirm. Both are
valid, but they are different screens.

## What the payer sees

Zapi returns a `checkout_url` for the payment. The payer approves the amount in
the provider's own flow: a telebirr prompt, a bank app, or a card form. Your
application never handles the payer's credentials.
