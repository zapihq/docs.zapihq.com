Zapi is a payment gateway for Ethiopian merchants: one API in front of telebirr
wallets, bank accounts, and cards. You create a payment, the payer confirms on
Zapi's hosted checkout, and the captured funds settle to your merchant balance.

A payment gateway is not a provider. A provider is the institution behind a
payment method: a bank, or a wallet provider such as telebirr. Zapi fronts
several of them so you integrate once.

## What the gateway covers

- **Payments.** Collect from telebirr, bank accounts, and cards. The bank
  providers are Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE), Awash Bank, Bank of
  Abyssinia (BOA), and Dashen Bank. The create request names the method; the
  payer supplies the phone number, bank account, or card on the hosted
  checkout.
- **Refunds.** Return all or part of a succeeded payment, more than once, up to
  the captured amount.
- **Payouts.** Send money from your Zapi balance to a recipient's bank account
  or telebirr wallet. Onboard a recipient once, then reference it by its saved
  `rcp_4k1q7c` identifier.
- **Virtual accounts.** The docs describe account identifiers that route
  inbound funds to a single merchant. The public contract does not expose an
  endpoint for them.
- **Events.** Subscribe a webhook endpoint to the ten payment, refund, and
  payout event names. Deliveries are at least once, with three attempts per
  event.
- **Idempotent retries.** Send an `Idempotency-Key` with every `POST`. A retry
  with the same key, method, path, and body applies the change once and returns
  the original response.
- **Test mode.** Build with `sk_test_` keys and `livemode: false`, choose
  simulated outcomes on the checkout, then switch to a live key.

## How a payment moves

A create request returns the payment in `processing` and a `checkout_url` when
the payer must approve it. The payer acts on the hosted page, Zapi sends an
event to your webhook endpoint, and you retrieve the payment to confirm its
final state. Settlement turns a captured payment into balance that payouts draw
on; settlement timing and fees are not published here.
